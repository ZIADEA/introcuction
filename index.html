Vibration Lab 1: Unbalance Identification 04/2026
ENSAM Meknès, predictive maintenance by AI module, team of 5
Identification of an unbalance on a power-transmission test bench using a stroboscope and spectral analysis.
  • Measurement of the rotation frequency (24.88 Hz for 25 Hz theoretical) and vibration levels on 3 axes; spectral peak at the rotation frequency, characteristic of unbalance.
  • Severity assessed according to ISO 10816: 6.15 mm/s in the radial direction, above the alarm threshold.
Keywords: Vibrations, Unbalance, FFT, ISO 10816
Vibration Lab 2: SKF 6206 Bearing Diagnosis 04/2026
ENSAM Meknès, predictive maintenance by AI module, team of 5
Diagnosis of a rolling bearing from FFT spectra and defect frequencies, then effect of a two-plane unbalance.
  • Computed the bearing defect frequencies (outer race 71.4 Hz, ball 92.4 Hz, inner race 108.6 Hz) and compared them with the spectra measured at 6 points; defect confirmed after disassembly (wear and corrosion).
  • With added unbalance, RMS velocity increased by 17 to 88 % depending on the measuring point, with a peak at the rotation frequency and its harmonics.
Keywords: Vibrations, Bearings, Diagnosis, FFT
